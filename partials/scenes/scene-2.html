A plate becomes a person

A plate reader never learns a name. But a plate is a key, and any list that also contains plates can be joined onto it. Learn how a join works, then search a whole town.

Try this
  1. In part A, press Join four times
  2. Switch to part B and click a camera
  3. Turn on filters and check the guesses

Part A explains the idea with one car. Part B does the same for the whole town.

Now join four lists onto one plate

This is the car from scene 1. Each card is a list that someone holds. Look for the highlighted row: it has the same key as our row. Everything here is made up, including the name.

Try an example

An invented town of 100 people and 12 cameras. Registry fields (name, age, sex) are exact. Two things are guesses: a "likely delivery driver" (guessed from driving pattern alone) and a "likely Hispanic" (guessed from surname and neighborhood, because race is on no registry record).
