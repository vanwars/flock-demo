What is documented

  • Documented In January 2024 the FTC ordered data broker X-Mode Social (now Outlogic) to stop sharing or selling sensitive location data, covering places such as medical and reproductive health clinics, places of worship, domestic abuse shelters, and protest sites, and to delete location data it had collected. FTC press release, Jan. 9, 2024
  • Documented EFF reported that Thomson Reuters' CLEAR product supplied ICE with plate-reader data collected by Vigilant Solutions, along with other records such as utility bills and employment records. Thomson Reuters announced a human rights review of the product. EFF Deeplinks, May 9, 2022
  • Documented The House passed the Fourth Amendment Is Not For Sale Act 219 to 199 in April 2024. It would bar the government from buying data about Americans that it would need a warrant to get directly, including location data. ACLU, Apr. 17, 2024 Its current status has not been confirmed here.
  • Hypothetical The matching of plate sightings to phone pings, the advertising IDs and the 100 people are invented. The scene shows why the join works, not what any specific vendor does.
