What is documented

North Carolina's rules are the baseline for the campuses on the map.

  • Documented North Carolina law lets state and local law enforcement agencies use plate readers only for law enforcement purposes (such as criminal investigations, felony warrants, missing persons, stolen vehicles), and not to enforce traffic violations. N.C.G.S. Article 3D
  • Documented Plate data may not be kept more than 90 days after it is captured, unless a preservation request or a search warrant applies. Plate data is confidential and not a public record. N.C.G.S. § 20-183.32
  • Documented Each agency must adopt a written policy before using a system. The policy has to cover data retention, sharing with other agencies, access, supervision, and audits. N.C.G.S. § 20-183.31
  • Hypothetical The six-week trips, the retention menu (30 days, 7 days, 1 day) and the guessed habits in this scene are invented. Your campus's real retention setting is a question on the map's campus cards.
