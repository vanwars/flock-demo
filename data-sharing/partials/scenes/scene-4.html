Then buy the rest

Once one advertising ID is tied to the plate, other purchased files that use the same ID can attach a phone number, a name, an address, shopping habits. Each file only needs to share one key with another.

What would limit this

  • Shorter retentionOn reads and on purchased location data: fewer sightings to line up.
  • Warrant or court-order rulesFor searching reads and for buying data about people.
  • Limits on sharingOutside the agency that owns the camera, and on who may search.
  • Search logsWith a specific reason and a case number, reviewed and published.
  • Vendor limitsNot collecting or selling precise phone locations in the first place.
