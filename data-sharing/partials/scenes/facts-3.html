What is documented

Search-log reasons are self-entered and some federal agencies have broad mandates beyond immigration, so a tag in a log is a lead, not proof.

  • Documented Denver police audit logs showed more than 1,400 immigration-related searches of the city's Flock data between June 2024 and April 2025, about 690 of them after January 2025. The reasons were typed by the searchers; in many cases they read "ICE" or "immigration". Most searchers were out-of-state departments. Denver turned off national searching in April 2025. Colorado Newsline via Colorado FOIC, Aug. 7, 2025
  • Documented A University of Washington report, based on audits from public-records requests, found at least 8 Washington agencies had enabled direct sharing with U.S. Border Patrol in 2025, and that Border Patrol searched the networks of at least 10 more that had not explicitly authorized it. Six agencies refused or partly refused to provide audits. UW Center for Human Rights, 2025
  • Documented A counter-example: a Columbus, Ohio audit of about 19.7 million searches (Nov. 2023 to June 2026) found zero searches by ICE itself. It found 3,962 searches by Border Patrol and Homeland Security Investigations, 657 of them with a possible immigration-related reason, The article also reports zero immigration-related searches by federal agencies in the first half of 2026, after the city added filters in late 2025. ABC6, July 14, 2026
  • Documented North Carolina law (HB 318) took effect Oct. 1, 2025. It requires sheriffs to hold certain undocumented inmates 48 hours past release and to notify ICE two hours before release. The article does not mention plate readers. WFAE, Oct. 1, 2025
