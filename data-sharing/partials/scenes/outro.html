What to do with this

The data is only as private as the rules around it.

Every scene came down to the same few questions. They are also the questions to put to a campus police department, in a public-records request or at a public meeting.

  • How long is it kept?Shorter retention means fewer sightings to line up.
  • Who can search it?And what reason must they give each time?
  • Who is it shared with?Other agencies, in this state or out of it.
  • Who checks the searches?Are the audit logs reviewed, and are they public?
