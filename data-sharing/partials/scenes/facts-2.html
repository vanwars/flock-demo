  • Documented A 2023–2026 audit of one Ohio city's Flock network covered 19,664,308 searches in about 32 months, which shows that searching camera data by place and time is routine at scale. ABC6, July 14, 2026
  • Documented In North Carolina, plate data is limited by statute to law enforcement purposes and to 90 days of retention (see scene 1). N.C.G.S. Article 3D
  • Documented The federal Driver's Privacy Protection Act bars state motor-vehicle agencies from disclosing personal information from a vehicle record, except for listed uses. 18 U.S.C. § 2721(a)
  • Documented The listed uses include use by any government agency, including courts and law enforcement, in carrying out its functions; insurers for claims, antifraud and underwriting; and licensed private investigators and security services. 18 U.S.C. § 2721(b)
  • Documented The federal consumer finance regulator (CFPB) has published a proxy method that estimates race and ethnicity from publicly available information for people whose race is not recorded, and tested it against mortgage applicants whose race is known. CFPB report page
